Our story

Ember was started in 2019 by two former baristas who wanted café-quality coffee to taste just as good at home. We roast in small 12 kg batches every Monday and ship the same week, so your coffee arrives at its best.

Why Monday

Coffee is at its best in the weeks after roasting. Roasting once a week, on Monday, and shipping only Tuesday to Thursday means no bag sits on a shelf: what you order is what we roast that week.

Small batches

Our roaster takes 12 kg at a time. Small batches let us roast each coffee to its own profile: light for our Colombian and Ethiopian single origins, so their floral and fruity notes come through, and dark for Midnight Blend, made for espresso and milk.

How we choose our coffees

We buy from importers who work directly with producers in Colombia, Ethiopia, Brazil and Guatemala, and we taste every lot before it joins our range. Some coffees, like Highland Yirgacheffe, are limited lots: when they're gone, they're gone until the next harvest.

For cafés and offices

We supply cafés and offices from 5 kg a month and train café teams to brew our coffees well. Write to wholesale@ember-coffee.example.
