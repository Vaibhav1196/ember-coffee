Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Write to hello@ember-coffee.example.

Which coffee should I choose for espresso?

Midnight Blend. Its body and dark chocolate notes work beautifully as espresso and in milk drinks like lattes and cappuccinos.

Which coffee is best for pour-over?

Sunrise Single Origin (citrusy and sweet) or our limited Highland Yirgacheffe (floral, jasmine and bergamot). Both are light roasts that shine as pour-over or filter.

Do you have decaf?

Yes: Velvet Decaf, a Colombian coffee decaffeinated with the chemical-free Swiss Water Process. It's 99.9% caffeine-free, with milk chocolate and caramel notes, and works for every brewing method. €13,50 for 250 g.

How fresh is the coffee?

We roast every Monday and ship Tuesday to Thursday. The roast date is printed on every bag. Coffee tastes best between one and six weeks after roasting.

Should I buy whole bean or ground?

Whole bean stays fresh longest; grind just before brewing if you can. If you don't have a grinder, choose your grind at checkout: espresso, moka pot, filter / pour-over or French press.

How should I store coffee?

Keep it in its resealable bag or an airtight container, somewhere cool and dark. Avoid the fridge. For longer than six weeks, freeze sealed portions and thaw only what you need.

How much caffeine is in a cup?

It varies with the coffee and recipe, but a typical 250 ml cup of filter coffee contains roughly 90 to 140 mg, and a single espresso around 60 to 80 mg. Velvet Decaf is 99.9% caffeine-free.

How does the subscription work?

The Ember Subscription sends two 250 g bags every month for €24,90, about 15% less than buying single bags, with free shipping. Choose your roasts or let us surprise you with the roaster's choice. Pause, skip or cancel anytime from your account.

Can I change my subscription?

Yes. You can change roasts, grind or delivery date anytime before your next roast day (Monday).

Do you sell to cafés and offices?

Yes, from 5 kg a month, with brewing training for café teams. Write to wholesale@ember-coffee.example.

Are your bags recyclable?

Our bags are made of paper with a plant-based lining and can go into industrial composting.

What if my order arrives damaged?

Send a photo to hello@ember-coffee.example within 7 days and we'll replace it for free.
