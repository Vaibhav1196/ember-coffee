Shipping and returns

When your order ships

We roast every Monday and ship Tuesday to Thursday of the same week, so your coffee arrives at its freshest. Orders placed after Thursday 12:00 ship the following week, freshly roasted. Equipment, merchandise and Cold Brew Packs ship within 2 business days. You'll receive a tracking link by email as soon as your parcel leaves the roastery.

The European Union zone covers every EU country, for example Germany, Belgium, Spain, Italy and the Netherlands. Free shipping applies in France above €35 and in the rest of the EU above €50. We don't ship outside Europe yet. Subscriptions always ship free within France and the European Union.

Returns

  • Unopened coffee: return it within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
  • Equipment and merchandise: return unused items in their original packaging within 30 days for a full refund.
  • Opened coffee: we can't take it back, but if a coffee isn't for you, write to us and we'll send a different one, once per customer (our Taste Guarantee).
  • Cold Brew Packs can't be returned once delivered, because they're perishable.

Return shipping is paid by the customer, except for damaged or wrong items.

Damaged or wrong items

Send a photo to hello@ember-coffee.example within 7 days of delivery and we'll send a replacement free of charge. No need to return the damaged item.

Refunds

Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5 business days of us receiving the return.
