Pour-over

Our pick: Sunrise Single Origin or Highland Yirgacheffe.

  1. Rinse the paper filter with hot water and discard the water.
  2. Add 15 g of coffee ground medium-fine, like table salt.
  3. Bloom: pour 30 g of water at 93 °C and wait 30 seconds.
  4. Pour slowly in small circles until the scale reads 250 g, by about 1:45.
  5. Let it drain. Total time should be 2:45 to 3:15. Too fast or sour? Grind finer. Too slow or bitter? Grind coarser.

Espresso

Our pick: Midnight Blend.

  1. Dose 18 g of finely ground coffee into the basket.
  2. Distribute evenly and tamp level.
  3. Brew 36 g of espresso in 27 to 30 seconds at 92 to 94 °C.
  4. Running fast and sour? Grind finer. Slow and bitter? Grind coarser.

French press

Our pick: Midnight Blend or Velvet Decaf.

  1. Add 30 g of coarsely ground coffee.
  2. Pour 500 g of water at 94 °C, stir gently and put the lid on.
  3. Wait 4 minutes, skim off the crust, then press slowly.
  4. Serve straight away so it doesn't keep brewing.

Moka pot

Our pick: Midnight Blend.

  1. Fill the base with hot water up to the valve.
  2. Fill the basket with fine-medium ground coffee and level it without tamping.
  3. Brew on medium-low heat with the lid open.
  4. Take it off the heat as soon as it starts to gurgle, and cool the base under the tap.

Cold brew at home

Our pick: Sunrise Single Origin. Short on time? Try our ready-to-drink Cold Brew Pack.

  1. Mix 100 g of coarsely ground coffee with 800 g of cold water.
  2. Steep in the fridge for 18 hours.
  3. Filter, then dilute 1:1 with water or milk.
  4. Keeps for 7 days in the fridge.
